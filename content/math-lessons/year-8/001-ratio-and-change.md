## 1. Simplifying Ratios and Ratio Notation

### What does this mean?
A **ratio** compares the relative sizes of two or more quantities. Ratios are written using the colon symbol ($:$), for example $a : b$. Simplifying a ratio means dividing all parts by their **highest common factor (HCF)** until no further whole-number division is possible.

![Diagram: Visual bar model showing 6 red blocks and 4 blue blocks simplified into 3 red blocks and 2 blue blocks representing 6:4 = 3:2](/maths-images/Y8/simplifying-ratios.webp)

---

### Method
1. Identify all numbers in the ratio.
2. Find the **highest common factor (HCF)** of all numbers in the ratio.
3. Divide each part of the ratio by the HCF.
4. If units are different, convert them to the **same unit** before simplifying.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Simplify the ratio $12\text{ cm} : 18\text{ cm} : 24\text{ cm}$.

**Answer:**
* Find the HCF of $12$, $18$, and $24$, which is $6$.
* Divide each term by $6$:
  * $12 \div 6 = 2$
  * $18 \div 6 = 3$
  * $24 \div 6 = 4$

Answer: **$2 : 3 : 4$**

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> Ratios must always be in the same units before simplifying! E.g., simplify $50\text{p} : \$2$ by first converting $\$2$ into $200\text{p}$ to get $50 : 200 = 1 : 4$.

## 2. Dividing an Amount into a Given Ratio

### What does this mean?
Sharing a total quantity into proportional parts specified by a ratio $a : b$.

![Diagram: Bar model showing total amount divided into equal blocks/parts corresponding to ratio parts](/maths-images/Y8/dividing-in-a-ratio.webp)

---

### Method
1. **Add** the ratio parts together to find the **total number of parts**.
2. **Divide** the total quantity by the number of parts to find the **value of one part**.
3. **Multiply** the value of one part by each ratio number.
4. **Check** that the individual answers add up to the original total.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Share $\$150$ in the ratio $2 : 3$.

**Answer:**
* Total parts $= 2 + 3 = 5\text{ parts}$
* Value of $1\text{ part} = \$150 \div 5 = \$30$
* Share $1 = 2 \times \$30 = \$60$
* Share $2 = 3 \times \$30 = \$90$

Check: $\$60 + \$90 = \$150$.

Answer: **$\$60 : \$90$**

## 3. Direct Proportion and Scale Drawings

### What does this mean?
Two quantities are in **direct proportion** if increasing one causes the other to increase at the exact same rate. A **scale drawing** uses direct proportion to represent real-life distances using a scale factor (e.g., $1\text{ cm} : 5\text{ m}$).

![Diagram: Map scale visual showing 1cm on map representing 5m in real life with a measurement line of 4cm](/maths-images/Y8/direct-proportion-and-scale-drawings.webp)

---

### Method
1. Find the **unitary value** (the value corresponding to $1$ unit) by dividing.
2. Multiply the unitary value by the required target quantity.
3. For scale drawings: $\text{Real distance} = \text{Scale distance} \times \text{Scale factor}$.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
A map has a scale of $1\text{ cm} : 4\text{ km}$. If two towns are $6.5\text{ cm}$ apart on the map, find the actual distance between them.

**Answer:**
* $1\text{ cm} = 4\text{ km}$
* $6.5\text{ cm} = 6.5 \times 4\text{ km} = 26\text{ km}$

Answer: **$26\text{ km}$**
