
## 1. Averages from Frequency Tables

### What does this mean?
Calculating the **mean**, **modal class**, and **median** from grouped or ungrouped frequency tables.

![Diagram: Grouped frequency table showing columns: Class Interval, Frequency, Midpoint (x), and Frequency x Midpoint (fx)](/maths-images/Y8/grouped-frequency-table.png)

---

### Method (Estimated Mean for Grouped Data):
1. Find the **midpoint ($x$)** for each class interval.
2. Calculate $f \times x$ for each row (Frequency $\times$ Midpoint).
3. Sum the total frequency column ($\sum f$).
4. Sum the total $fx$ column ($\sum fx$).
5. Calculate estimated mean:

$$\text{Estimated Mean} = \frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}$$

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
A survey of test scores gives $\sum f = 20$ and $\sum fx = 280$. Calculate the estimated mean score.

**Answer:**
$$\text{Estimated Mean} = \frac{280}{20} = 14$$

Answer: **$14$**

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> When working with grouped frequency tables, remember that you are calculating an **estimated mean** because the exact values inside each interval are unknown!
