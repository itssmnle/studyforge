## 1. Operations with Decimals

### What does this mean?
Performing addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division with decimal numbers while maintaining place value accuracy.

![Diagram: Decimal addition grid aligning 12.45 and 3.80 vertically at the decimal point](/maths-images/Y7/11-operations-with-decimals.webp)

---

### Method

#### Addition & Subtraction:
1. Align the numbers vertically by their **decimal points**.
2. Fill empty spaces with zeros.
3. Add or subtract as normal, keeping the decimal point in the same position in your answer.

#### Multiplying Decimals:
1. Ignore the decimal points and multiply the numbers as whole integers.
2. Count the total number of decimal places in the original question.
3. Place the decimal point in the answer so it has the same total number of decimal places.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Calculate $3.2 \times 0.04$.

**Answer:**
1. Ignore decimals: $32 \times 4 = 128$.
2. Count decimal places: $3.2$ ($1$ place) + $0.04$ ($2$ places) = $3$ total decimal places.
3. Move decimal point $3$ places left in $128 \rightarrow 0.128$.

Answer: **$0.128$**

## 2. Financial Maths: Bills and Bank Statements

### What does this mean?
Financial maths involves calculating total costs, itemised bills, change, running balances on bank statements, and savings.

![Diagram: Bank statement table showing columns for Date, Description, Credit, Debit, and Balance](/maths-images/Y7/12-financial-maths-bank-statement.webp)

---

### Method
* **Credit (In / Paid in):** Money added to an account ($\text{Balance} + \text{Credit}$).
* **Debit (Out / Paid out):** Money taken out of an account ($\text{Balance} - \text{Debit}$).
* **Balance:** The total amount of money in the account at any given time.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
A bank account has a starting balance of $\$150.00$. A payment of $\$45.50$ is deposited (credit) and a bill of $\$82.20$ is paid (debit). Find the final balance.

**Answer:**
1. Add credit: $\$150.00 + \$45.50 = \$195.50$
2. Subtract debit: $\$195.50 - \$82.20 = \$113.30$

Answer: **$\$113.30$**

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> Always write money answers to exactly $2$ decimal places (e.g., write $\$113.30$, not $\$113.3$).
