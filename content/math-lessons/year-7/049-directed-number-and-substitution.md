## 1. Operations with Negative Numbers

### What does this mean?
Directed numbers are numbers that have both a size and a direction (positive or negative).

---

### Method

#### Addition & Subtraction (Two signs meeting):
* $+ + \rightarrow +$
* $- - \rightarrow +$
* $+ - \rightarrow -$
* $- + \rightarrow -$

#### Multiplication & Division:
* Same signs result in a **positive** answer.
* Different signs result in a **negative** answer.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Calculate: 1) $-4 - (-7)$, 2) $-3 \times 5$

**Answer:**
1. $- - \rightarrow + \implies -4 + 7 = \mathbf{3}$
2. Different signs $\rightarrow$ negative $\implies -3 \times 5 = \mathbf{-15}$

---

## 2. Substitution with Negatives

### What does this mean?
Replacing variables in an algebraic expression with given negative numbers.

---

### Method
1. Put negative numbers inside **brackets** when substituting.
2. Evaluate powers first (remember a negative number squared becomes **positive**!).

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Evaluate $a^2 - 3b$ when $a = -3$ and $b = -2$.

**Answer:**
1. Substitute with brackets: $(-3)^2 - 3(-2)$
2. $(-3)^2 = +9$
3. $-3(-2) = +6$
4. Total: $9 + 6 = 15$

Answer: **$15$**

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> Be careful with $(-3)^2$ vs $-3^2$! $(-3)^2 = (-3) \times (-3) = +9$, whereas $-3^2 = -(3 \times 3) = -9$. Always use brackets when squaring negative numbers.
