## 1. Mean, Median, Mode, and Range

### What does this mean?
Measures of central tendency (averages) and spread (range) used to summarise datasets.

---

### Method
* **Mean:** $\frac{\text{Sum of all values}}{\text{Total number of values}}$
* **Median:** Middle value when numbers are ordered from smallest to largest.
* **Mode:** The value that appears **most frequently**.
* **Range:** Difference between largest and smallest values ($\text{Highest} - \text{Lowest}$).

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Find the mean and median of: $3, 7, 2, 9, 4$.

**Answer:**
* **Mean:** $\frac{3 + 7 + 2 + 9 + 4}{5} = \frac{25}{5} = \mathbf{5}$
* **Median:**
  1. Order data: $2, 3, 4, 7, 9$
  2. Middle number is $4$.
  Answer: **$4$**
