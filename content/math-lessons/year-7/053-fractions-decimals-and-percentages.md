## 1. Converting Between FDP

### What does this mean?
Fractions, decimals, and percentages are different ways of expressing the same proportional value.

![Diagram: Equivalence table showing standard FDP conversions like 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%, 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%](/maths-images/Y7/16-converting-between-fdp.webp)

---

### Method
* **Fraction $\rightarrow$ Decimal:** Divide top number (numerator) by bottom number (denominator).
* **Decimal $\rightarrow$ Percentage:** Multiply by $100$.
* **Percentage $\rightarrow$ Fraction:** Write over $100$ and simplify.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Convert $\frac{3}{5}$ into a decimal and a percentage.

**Answer:**
* Decimal: $3 \div 5 = \mathbf{0.6}$
* Percentage: $0.6 \times 100 = \mathbf{60\%}$

## 2. Operations with Fractions

### What does this mean?
Adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing proper fractions and mixed numbers.

---

### Method

#### Addition & Subtraction:
1. Find a **Common Denominator** (LCM).
2. Convert numerators proportionally.
3. Add or subtract numerators, keep denominator the same.

#### Multiplication:
Multiply numerators together, multiply denominators together ($\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ac}{bd}$).

#### Division:
**Keep, Change, Flip (KCF):** Keep first fraction, change $\div$ to $\times$, flip second fraction.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Calculate $\frac{2}{3} \div \frac{4}{5}$.

**Answer:**
1. Keep $\frac{2}{3}$, Change $\div \rightarrow \times$, Flip $\frac{4}{5} \rightarrow \frac{5}{4}$.
2. $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{5}{4} = \frac{10}{12}$
3. Simplify: $\frac{10}{12} = \frac{5}{6}$

Answer: **$\frac{5}{6}$**
