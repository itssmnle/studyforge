## 1. Estimation and Mental Strategies

### What does this mean?
Using rounding and mental math techniques to approximate answers and quickly check calculations.

---

### Method
1. Round every number in the calculation to **$1$ significant figure ($1\text{ sf}$)**.
2. Perform the simplified calculation.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Estimate the value of $\frac{402 \times 5.91}{0.48}$.

**Answer:**
1. Round to $1\text{ sf}$:
   * $402 \rightarrow 400$
   * $5.91 \rightarrow 6$
   * $0.48 \rightarrow 0.5$
2. Calculate numerator: $400 \times 6 = 2400$
3. Divide by $0.5$: $2400 \div 0.5 = 4800$

Answer: **$4800$**
