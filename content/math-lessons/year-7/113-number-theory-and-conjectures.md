## 1. Prime Factor Trees, HCF, and LCM

### What does this mean?
Breaking numbers down into their prime factors to calculate the **Highest Common Factor (HCF)** and **Lowest Common Multiple (LCM)** using Venn diagrams.

![Diagram: Venn diagram showing prime factors of 12 (2, 2, 3) and 18 (2, 3, 3) with intersection containing shared prime factors 2 and 3](/maths-images/Y7/20-prime-factor-trees-hcf-lcm.png)

---

### Method
1. Draw prime factor trees to write numbers as products of prime factors.
2. Place shared prime factors in the **intersection** of a Venn diagram.
3. Place remaining prime factors in their respective sets.
4. **HCF:** Multiply prime factors in the **intersection**.
5. **LCM:** Multiply **all** prime factors across the entire Venn diagram.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:** Find the HCF and LCM of $12$ and $18$.

**Answer:**
* $12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$
* $18 = 2 \times 3 \times 3$
* Intersection (shared): $2, 3 \rightarrow \text{HCF} = 2 \times 3 = \mathbf{6}$
* All numbers in Venn diagram: $2, 2, 3, 3 \rightarrow \text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = \mathbf{36}$

Answer: **$\text{HCF} = 6$, $\text{LCM} = 36$**
