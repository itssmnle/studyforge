## 1. Pythagoras' Theorem in 3D

### What does this mean?
Extending Pythagoras' theorem ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$) into three dimensions to calculate space diagonals inside 3D boxes/cuboids.

$$d^2 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \quad \implies \quad d = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$$

![Diagram: 3D cuboid with length x, width y, and height z showing space diagonal d passing through the center](/maths-images/Y9/cuboid-space-diagonal.webp)

---

### Method
1. Square the length, width, and height of the cuboid.
2. Add the three squared numbers together.
3. Take the square root of the total sum.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Find the internal diagonal $d$ of a box with dimensions $3\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} \times 12\text{ cm}$.

**Answer:**
1. $d^2 = 3^2 + 4^2 + 12^2 = 9 + 16 + 144 = 169$
2. $d = \sqrt{169} = 13\text{ cm}$

Answer: **$13\text{ cm}$**

## 2. Right-Angled Trigonometry (SOH CAH TOA)

### What does this mean?
Trigonometric ratios connect side lengths and acute angles in right-angled triangles:

$$\sin(\theta) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} \quad \Big| \quad \cos(\theta) = \frac{\text{Adjacent}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} \quad \Big| \quad \tan(\theta) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}}$$

![Diagram: Right-angled triangle showing Hypotenuse (opposite 90 angle), Opposite side (opposite angle theta), and Adjacent side (next to theta)](/maths-images/Y9/trigonometry-triangle-sides.webp)

---

### Method
1. Label sides relative to target angle $\theta$: **Hypotenuse (H)**, **Opposite (O)**, **Adjacent (A)**.
2. Select correct ratio formula (**SOH**, **CAH**, or **TOA**).
3. To find a missing side: multiply or divide as required.
4. To find a missing angle: use inverse functions ($\sin^{-1}, \cos^{-1}, \tan^{-1}$).

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
A right-angled triangle has an angle of $30^\circ$ and a hypotenuse of $10\text{ cm}$. Find the opposite side $x$.

**Answer:**
1. Given $\theta = 30^\circ$, $\text{H} = 10$, want $\text{O} = x \rightarrow$ Use **SOH** ($\sin$).
2. $\sin(30^\circ) = \frac{x}{10}$
3. $x = 10 \times \sin(30^\circ) = 10 \times 0.5 = 5\text{ cm}$

Answer: **$5\text{ cm}$**
