## 1. Volume and Surface Area of Prisms and Cylinders

### What does this mean?
* **Prism:** A 3D shape with a constant cross-sectional area along its length.
* **Volume:** Space occupied inside the 3D shape.
* **Surface Area:** Total area of all 2D outer faces combined.

![Diagram: Cylinder showing circular cross-sectional base area pi*r^2 and height h, alongside triangular prism](/maths-images/Y9/cylinder-and-triangular-prism-cross-sections.png)

---

### Method

#### Volume of any Prism:
$$\text{Volume} = \text{Cross-sectional Area} \times \text{Length}$$

#### Volume of Cylinder:
$$\text{Volume} = \pi r^2 h$$

#### Surface Area of Cylinder:
$$\text{Surface Area} = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h$$

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Calculate the volume of a cylinder with radius $3\text{ cm}$ and height $10\text{ cm}$. Give your answer in terms of $\pi$.

**Answer:**
$$\text{Volume} = \pi \times 3^2 \times 10 = \pi \times 9 \times 10 = 90\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

Answer: **$90\pi\text{ cm}^3$**

## 2. Spheres, Cones, and Pyramids

### What does this mean?
Calculating volumes for non-prism curved and pointed 3D solids using standard formulas:

* **Volume of Cone / Pyramid:**

$$\text{Volume} = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times h$$

* **Volume of Sphere:**

$$\text{Volume} = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$

![Diagram: 3D Cone showing perpendicular height h, slant height l, and base radius r](/maths-images/Y9/cone-height-slant-radius.png "Cone dimensions: labelled side view")
![Diagram: Alternate 3D cone showing perpendicular height h, slant height l, and base radius r](/maths-images/Y9/cone-height-slant-radius2.png "Cone dimensions: alternate view")

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Find the volume of a sphere with a radius of $6\text{ cm}$. Give your answer in terms of $\pi$.

**Answer:**
$$\text{Volume} = \frac{4}{3} \times \pi \times 6^3 = \frac{4}{3} \times \pi \times 216 = 288\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

Answer: **$288\pi\text{ cm}^3$**
