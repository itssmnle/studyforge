## 1. Congruence Criteria for Triangles

### What does this mean?
Two shapes are **congruent** if they are identical in both shape and size. For triangles, four specific conditions prove congruence:

1. **SSS:** Side, Side, Side (all three sides equal).
2. **SAS:** Side, Angle, Side (two sides and the included angle equal).
3. **ASA:** Angle, Side, Angle (two angles and the included side equal).
4. **RHS:** Right angle, Hypotenuse, Side.

![Diagram: Four pairs of congruent triangles illustrating SSS, SAS, ASA, and RHS congruence rules](/maths-images/Y9/triangle-congruence-rules.webp)

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Triangle $A$ has sides $5\text{ cm}, 7\text{ cm}$ and an included angle of $40^\circ$. Triangle $B$ has sides $5\text{ cm}, 7\text{ cm}$ and an included angle of $40^\circ$. Are they congruent?

**Answer:**
Yes, because two sides and the included angle are identical.

Answer: **Congruent (Reason: SAS)**

## 2. Similarity and Area/Volume Scale Factors

### What does this mean?
Two shapes are **similar** if they have the same shape but different sizes (corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are in proportion).

* **Linear Scale Factor ($k$):** Length scale factor
* **Area Scale Factor ($k^2$):** Area increases by $k^2$
* **Volume Scale Factor ($k^3$):** Volume increases by $k^3$

![Diagram: Two similar 3D prisms showing linear scale factor k = 2, area scale factor k^2 = 4, and volume scale factor k^3 = 8](/maths-images/Y9/prism-scale-factors.webp)

---

### Method
1. Find linear scale factor $k = \frac{\text{New Length}}{\text{Old Length}}$.
2. For area problems, square the scale factor ($k^2$).
3. For volume problems, cube the scale factor ($k^3$).

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Two similar cylinders have heights of $3\text{ cm}$ and $6\text{ cm}$. If the smaller cylinder has a volume of $15\text{ cm}^3$, find the volume of the larger cylinder.

**Answer:**
1. Linear scale factor $k = \frac{6}{3} = 2$
2. Volume scale factor $k^3 = 2^3 = 8$
3. $\text{Larger Volume} = 15 \times 8 = 120\text{ cm}^3$

Answer: **$120\text{ cm}^3$**
