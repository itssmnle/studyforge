## 1. Energy Stores, Transfers, and Work Done

### What does this mean?
The **Law of Conservation of Energy** states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred between stores:

* **Kinetic Energy ($E_k$):** $E_k = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$
* **Gravitational Potential Energy ($\Delta E_p$):** $\Delta E_p = m g h$
* **Work Done ($W$):** Energy transferred when a force moves an object through a distance.
* Common energy stores include kinetic, gravitational, elastic, chemical, thermal, magnetic, electrostatic, and nuclear stores.
* Transfers occur mechanically, electrically, by heating, or by radiation.

$$W = F \times d$$

### Method: Energy Conservation Problems
Assuming no energy is lost to thermal friction:

$$\text{Loss of } E_p = \text{Gain in } E_k \quad \implies \quad m g h = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$$

---

### Worked Example & Answer

**Question:**
A ball of mass $0.5\text{ kg}$ is dropped from a cliff height of $20\text{ m}$. Calculate its kinetic energy and speed just before hitting the ground ($g = 10\text{ N/kg}$).

**Answer:**
1. Calculate $E_p$ at top:
   $$E_p = mgh = 0.5 \times 10 \times 20 = 100\text{ J}$$
2. Energy transferred to $E_k$:
   $$E_k = 100\text{ J}$$
3. Calculate speed $v$:
   $$100 = \frac{1}{2} \times 0.5 \times v^2 \implies 100 = 0.25 v^2$$
   $$v^2 = \frac{100}{0.25} = 400 \implies v = \sqrt{400} = 20\text{ m/s}$$

Answer: **$E_k = 100\text{ J}$, $v = 20\text{ m/s}$**

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> $1\text{ Joule } (1\text{ J})$ of Work Done is equal to a force of $1\text{ Newton } (1\text{ N})$ moving an object through a distance of $1\text{ metre } (1\text{ m})$.
