## 1. Cell Structure and Function

### What does this mean?
Cells are the basic structural and functional units of all living organisms.
* **Animal Cells:** Contain nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria, and ribosomes.
* **Plant Cells:** Contain all animal cell structures plus a **cell wall**, **permanent vacuole**, and **chloroplasts**.
* **Bacterial Cells:** Prokaryotes that lack a nucleus; DNA is floating free in cytoplasm alongside plasmids and a peptidoglycan cell wall.

Key functions include: nucleus controls cell activities and contains DNA; cytoplasm is where reactions occur; cell membrane controls entry and exit; mitochondria carry out aerobic respiration; ribosomes make proteins; cell wall supports the cell; chloroplasts absorb light; and the vacuole contains cell sap and helps keep plant cells turgid.

### Method: Calculating Magnification

$$\text{Magnification} = \frac{\text{Image Size}}{\text{Actual Size}} \quad \implies \quad I = A \times M$$

1. Measure the image size ($I$) using a ruler in **millimetres ($\text{mm}$)**.
2. Convert $\text{mm}$ to **micrometres ($\mu\text{m}$)** by multiplying by $1000$.
3. Divide image size by actual size or magnification.

---

### Worked Example & Answer

**Question:**
An image of a cell measures $24\text{ mm}$ under a microscope. The actual size of the cell is $60\text{ }\mu\text{m}$. Calculate the magnification.

**Answer:**
1. Convert image size to $\mu\text{m}$: $24\text{ mm} \times 1000 = 24,000\text{ }\mu\text{m}$
2. Calculate magnification:
   $$\text{Magnification} = \frac{24,000}{60} = 400$$

Answer: **$\times 400$**

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> Always check your units before calculating! Remember the conversion: $1\text{ mm} = 1000\text{ }\mu\text{m}$. Always multiply $\text{mm}$ by $1000$ to convert to $\mu\text{m}$ before using the triangle formula.

## 2. Cell Division, Specialisation, and Organisation

### What does this mean?
New body cells are produced by **mitosis**. Before division, the genetic material is copied; one cell then divides to form two genetically identical daughter cells. Cells become specialised by developing structures suited to a function.

* **Red blood cell:** Biconcave shape and no nucleus provide more space for haemoglobin.
* **Root hair cell:** Long extension gives a large surface area for absorbing water and mineral ions.
* **Ciliated cell:** Cilia move mucus and trapped pathogens out of the airways.
* **Palisade cell:** Many chloroplasts absorb light for photosynthesis.

Organisation follows this hierarchy:
$$\text{cell} \rightarrow \text{tissue} \rightarrow \text{organ} \rightarrow \text{organ system} \rightarrow \text{organism}$$

### Method
1. Identify the structure visible in the cell.
2. Link that structure to an advantage.
3. State how the advantage supports the cell's function.

### Worked Example & Answer

**Question:** Explain how a root hair cell is adapted for absorbing mineral ions.

**Answer:** Its long projection provides a large surface area for absorption. It also contains many mitochondria, which release energy for active transport of mineral ions.

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> Do not only name an adaptation. Link **structure → advantage → function**.
