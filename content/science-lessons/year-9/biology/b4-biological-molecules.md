## 1. Food Tests and Nutrients

### What does this mean?
Biological molecules are made from chemical elements:
* **Carbohydrates & Fats:** Carbon ($\text{C}$), Hydrogen ($\text{H}$), Oxygen ($\text{O}$).
* **Proteins:** Carbon ($\text{C}$), Hydrogen ($\text{H}$), Oxygen ($\text{O}$), and Nitrogen ($\text{N}$).

Large molecules are built from smaller units: starch and glycogen from glucose, proteins from amino acids, and fats from fatty acids plus glycerol.

### Method: Biochemical Food Tests
1. **Starch:** Add **Iodine solution** $\rightarrow$ Turns from yellow-brown to **blue-black**.
2. **Reducing Sugars (Glucose):** Add **Benedict's reagent** and **heat in a water bath** ($80^\circ\text{C}$) $\rightarrow$ Turns from blue to **brick-red precipitate**.
3. **Protein:** Add **Biuret reagent** $\rightarrow$ Turns from blue to **purple / violet**.
4. **Fats / Lipids:** Mix with **Ethanol**, pour into water $\rightarrow$ Forms a **cloudy white emulsion**.

---

### Worked Example & Answer

**Question:**
A student tests a unknown solution with Biuret reagent and it turns purple. What biological molecule is present?

**Answer:**
The purple colour change with Biuret reagent indicates the presence of protein.

Answer: **Protein**

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> Give both the reagent and the positive result. For Benedict's test, heating is required.
