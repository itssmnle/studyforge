## 1. Types of Forces and Hooke's Law

### What does this mean?
A **force** is a push, pull, or turn acting on an object, measured in Newtons ($\text{N}$) using a spring balance (newtonmeter).

![Diagram: Hooke's Law graph showing linear force vs extension relationship up to the limit of proportionality]

---

### Key Concepts & Formulae

#### Force Categories:
* **Contact Forces:** Friction, Air Resistance (Drag), Tension, Normal Reaction, Upthrust.
* **Non-Contact Forces:** Gravity (Weight), Electrostatic Force, Magnetic Force.

#### Hooke's Law:
The extension of an elastic material is directly proportional to the applied force, up to the **limit of proportionality**:

$$\text{Force (N)} = \text{Spring Constant (N/m)} \times \text{Extension (m)} \quad \Big| \quad F = k \times e$$

$$\text{Work Done (J)} = \text{Force (N)} \times \text{Distance (m)} \quad \Big| \quad W = F \times d$$

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
A spring with a spring constant $k = 50\text{ N/m}$ is stretched by a force of $15\text{ N}$. Calculate its extension in centimetres.

**Answer:**
1. Calculate extension in metres: $e = \frac{F}{k} = \frac{15}{50} = 0.30\text{ m}$
2. Convert to $\text{cm}$: $0.30\text{ m} \times 100 = 30\text{ cm}$

Answer: **$30\text{ cm}$**

## 2. Balanced vs Unbalanced Forces

### What does this mean?
The **resultant force** is the single overall force acting on an object after combining all individual force vectors.

---

### Motion Rules:
* **Balanced Forces ($\text{Resultant Force} = 0\text{ N}$):**
  * If stationary, the object **remains stationary**.
  * If moving, the object continues moving at a **constant velocity** in a straight line (Newton's First Law).
* **Unbalanced Forces ($\text{Resultant Force} > 0\text{ N}$):**
  * The object **accelerates**, **decelerates**, or **changes direction**.
