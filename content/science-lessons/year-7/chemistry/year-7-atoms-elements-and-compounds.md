## 1. Atoms, Elements, Compounds, and Formulae

### What does this mean?
All matter in the universe is made from chemical building blocks called **atoms**.
* **Atom:** The smallest neutral particle of a chemical element.
* **Element:** A pure substance made of only **one type of atom** (e.g. Carbon $\text{C}$, Oxygen $\text{O}_2$, Gold $\text{Au}$).
* **Compound:** A pure substance formed when two or more **different elements are chemically bonded** together in fixed ratios (e.g. Water $\text{H}_2\text{O}$, Carbon Dioxide $\text{CO}_2$).
* **Mixture:** Two or more substances mixed together physically that are **not** chemically bonded.

### Key Concepts & Formulae Interpretation
* **Chemical Symbols:** Represent elements on the Periodic Table (always starts with a capital letter, e.g. $\text{Na}, \text{Cl}, \text{Fe}$).
* **Chemical Formulae:** Subscripts show the exact number of atoms of each element in a molecule/compound.

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Calculate the total number of atoms and the number of different elements present in one molecule of Sulfuric Acid ($\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$).

**Answer:**
* Elements present: Hydrogen ($\text{H}$), Sulfur ($\text{S}$), Oxygen ($\text{O}$) $\rightarrow$ **$3$ different elements**.
* Atom count: $2\text{ H} + 1\text{ S} + 4\text{ O} = 7\text{ total atoms}$.

Answer: **$3$ elements, $7$ total atoms**
