## 1. Circuit Symbols, Current, Voltage, and Resistance

### What does this mean?
Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge around a closed conductive circuit.

$$\text{Voltage } (V) = \text{Current } (I) \times \text{Resistance } (R) \quad \Big| \quad V = I R \quad \Big| \quad R = \frac{V}{I}$$

## 2. Series vs Parallel Circuits

### Circuit Rules:
* **Series Circuits:**
  * Current is the **SAME** at all points in the loop ($I_1 = I_2 = I_3$).
  * Total voltage is **SHARED** across components ($V_{\text{total}} = V_1 + V_2$).
* **Parallel Circuits:**
  * Voltage is the **SAME** across every parallel branch ($V_{\text{branch}} = V_{\text{total}}$).
  * Total current **SPLITS** at junctions ($I_{\text{total}} = I_1 + I_2$).

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
A resistor in a circuit has a voltage of $9\text{ V}$ across it and a current of $1.5\text{ A}$ flowing through it. Calculate its electrical resistance.

**Answer:**
$$R = \frac{V}{I} = \frac{9\text{ V}}{1.5\text{ A}} = 6\ \Omega$$

Answer: **$6\ \Omega$**
