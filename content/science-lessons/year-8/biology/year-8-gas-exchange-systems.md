## 1. Ventilation and Gas Exchange

### What does this mean?
**Ventilation** is the physical movement of air into and out of the lungs. **Gas exchange** is the diffusion of oxygen into the blood and carbon dioxide out of the blood across the alveoli walls.

### Mechanics of Breathing

| Feature | Inhalation (Breathing In) | Exhalation (Breathing Out) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diaphragm** | Contracts and flattens **downward** | Relaxes and arches **upward** |
| **Intercostal Muscles** | Contract, pulling ribcage **up and out** | Relax, dropping ribcage **down and in** |
| **Thorax Volume** | **Increases** | **Decreases** |
| **Thoracic Pressure** | **Decreases** below atmospheric pressure | **Increases** above atmospheric pressure |
| **Air Movement** | Rushes **INTO** lungs | Forced **OUT OF** lungs |

## 2. Alveoli Adaptations and Smoking Impacts

### Key Adaptations of Alveoli:
* **Millions of alveoli** provide an enormous total surface area.
* **Thin walls:** Alveolus wall and capillary wall are each only $1$ cell thick.
* **Moist lining:** Allows gases to dissolve before diffusing.
* **Extensive capillary network:** Maintains steep concentration gradients for $\text{O}_2$ and $\text{CO}_2$.

#### Harmful Effects of Smoking:
* **Tar:** Destroys **ciliated epithelial cells**, causing mucus accumulation ("smoker's cough") and bronchitis; contains **carcinogens** causing lung cancer.
* **Carbon Monoxide ($\text{CO}$):** Binds permanently to hemoglobin, reducing blood oxygen-carrying capacity.
* **Nicotine:** Addictive substance that increases heart rate and narrows blood vessels.
