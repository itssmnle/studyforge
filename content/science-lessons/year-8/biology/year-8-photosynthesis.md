## 1. Photosynthesis Equation and Leaf Adaptations

### What does this mean?
**Photosynthesis** is an **endothermic reaction** in which green plants absorb light energy using chlorophyll to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.

$$\text{Carbon Dioxide} + \text{Water} \xrightarrow[\text{chlorophyll}]{\text{light}} \text{Glucose} + \text{Oxygen}$$

$$6\text{CO}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2$$

### Leaf Structural Adaptations:
* **Palisade Mesophyll:** Columnar cells near leaf top packed with **chloroplasts** for maximum light absorption.
* **Spongy Mesophyll:** Loose arrangement with large **air spaces** to allow rapid diffusion of $\text{CO}_2$ and $\text{O}_2$.
* **Stomata & Guard Cells:** Microscopic pores on leaf underside; guard cells open/close stomata to regulate gas exchange and transpiration water loss.
* **Xylem:** Transport vessels supplying water and mineral ions to leaf cells.

## 2. Testing a Leaf for Starch

### Method
1. Dip leaf in boiling water for $1\text{ minute}$ (kills leaf cells and breaks cell membranes).
2. Boil leaf in **ethanol** using an **electric water bath** (removes green chlorophyll).
3. Rinse leaf in warm water (softens leaf).
4. Spread on white tile and add **Iodine solution**.
5. Positive result: Color changes from yellow-brown to **blue-black**.

> [!INFO] **Examiner Tips and Tricks**
> Never heat ethanol over an open Bunsen burner flame because ethanol is **extremely flammable**! Always use a hot water bath.
