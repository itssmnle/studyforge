## 1. Chemical Reaction Types and Conservation of Mass

### What does this mean?
A **chemical reaction** involves rearranging atoms to form new chemical substances with different properties.

---

### Key Reaction Types:
* **Combustion:** Reaction of a fuel with oxygen releasing heat ($\text{Fuel} + \text{Oxygen} \rightarrow \text{Carbon Dioxide} + \text{Water}$).
* **Thermal Decomposition:** Breaking down a compound using heat:

$$\text{Calcium Carbonate } (\text{CaCO}_3) \xrightarrow{\text{heat}} \text{Calcium Oxide } (\text{CaO}) + \text{Carbon Dioxide } (\text{CO}_2)$$

* **Displacement:** A more reactive element displaces a less reactive element from its compound solution.
* **Conservation of Mass:** Total mass of reactants **equals** total mass of products in any chemical reaction.

---

## 2. Acids, Alkalis, and Neutralisation

### What does this mean?
* **Acids:** Produce $\text{H}^+$ ions in aqueous solution ($\text{pH} < 7$). Turn Universal Indicator **red/orange/yellow**.
* **Alkalis:** Soluble bases producing $\text{OH}^-$ ions in solution ($\text{pH} > 7$). Turn Universal Indicator **blue/purple**.
* **Neutralisation:** Reaction between acid and alkali forming neutral salt and water ($\text{pH} = 7$):

$$\text{Acid} + \text{Alkali} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Water}$$

$$\text{H}^+ + \text{OH}^- \rightarrow \text{H}_2\text{O}$$

#### General Acid Reactions:
1. $\text{Acid} + \text{Metal} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Hydrogen}$ *(Tested with a burning splint $\rightarrow$ **squeaky pop**)*
2. $\text{Acid} + \text{Metal Carbonate} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Water} + \text{Carbon Dioxide}$ *(Tested with **limewater**)*

---

### Worked Example

**Question:**
Name the salt formed when magnesium reacts with nitric acid.

**Answer:**
Nitric acid forms nitrate salts $\rightarrow$ **Magnesium nitrate**.
